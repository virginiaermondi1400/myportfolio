02 / ABOUT ME

Between technology and imagination

To me, virtual reality is both a time machine and a means of teleportation: it lets us step into places, eras, and worlds otherwise beyond our reach. Creating these experiences feels a little like magic. Through code, imagery, sound, and interaction, I can craft small spells that transport people elsewhere.

I'm a software developer specializing in XR, with a passion for technology, art, and literature. I've never felt at home in rigid categories: I'm fascinated by the art behind technology and the technology behind art. My background brings together engineering, cinema, media, and the humanities.

I use Unity and C# to bring immersive experiences to life, from application logic to interactions and sound design. I enjoy working with people across disciplines, taking responsibility, and leading projects when needed—from the first idea to an experience people can actually step into.

A multidisciplinary background

Electronic and Communications Engineering · Cinema and Media Engineering · Arts, Music and Entertainment · Classical studies · Research experience at Ontario Tech University, Canada.
