Breastfeeding VR

Master’s thesis on interaction in virtual reality.

Meta Quest Pro · HCI
My contribution

For my master's thesis abroad at Ontario Tech University in Canada, I developed a VR application for learning breastfeeding latch techniques. I compared three interaction methods—hand tracking, controllers, and a realistic physical proxy—through tests assessing usability (SUS), workload (NASA-TLX), and perceived presence (PQ) to evaluate the strengths of each approach.
